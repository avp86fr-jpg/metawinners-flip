How to play

  1. Hit to flip a card into your line. Numbers add to your round score.
  2. Stay to bank your line. Bust = duplicate number = 0 this round.
  3. Joker (MetaWinners 1/1 face) = Second Chance: discards a duplicate once and saves you.
  4. Freeze forces someone to bank. Flip Three dumps 3 cards on a target.
  5. Modifiers (+N, ×2) boost scoring. ×2 only doubles number totals.
  6. Seven unique numbers = Flip 7 (+15) and the round ends now.
  7. First to 200 wins. Vs bots or local pass-and-play — no online play.
  8. HOUSE: MetaWinners 1/1 Joker art & portraits — same Second Chance rule, no extra powers.
